ASE Explorer – Support

ASE Explorer shows live data from the Amman Stock Exchange, with a watchlist and a personal portfolio tracker, in English and Arabic.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, email support@timsa7.com. Please include your device model and the app version from your phone's App Store or Google Play page.

Common questions

Where does the data come from? All market data comes from the Amman Stock Exchange website, exchange.jo. Prices can be delayed and are for information only, not investment advice.

I updated from the old app. Where are my favorites? Stocks you starred in the previous version appear on the Watchlist tab after the update. If some are missing, the company may have been renamed or delisted.

Is my portfolio sent anywhere? No. Your watchlist and portfolio stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

How do I switch to Arabic? Open Settings → Language and choose عربي. The app then asks you to reopen it so the layout switches to right-to-left.

أسئلة شائعة
