Super Trix Calculator – Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · applies to Super Trix Calculator (called Trix Calculator on your device) 1.0 and later on iOS and Android

Super Trix Calculator ("the app") keeps score for the card game Trix. It is published by Feras Arabiat ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

Information that stays on your device

You do not need an account, and the app never asks for your email address or phone number. The player names you type, the scores and your saved games are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. If you back up your device, the backup can include them.

Usage analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To understand how the app is used and improve it, the app sends usage events to Google Firebase Analytics. These are:

  • which screens you open;
  • that you started, finished or deleted a game, saved a contract or cleared History;
  • with those events: the game mode (individual or partners, Complex Scoring on or off), the contract and kingdom number, whether the King or Queens were doubled, and whether a deleted game was finished;
  • a change of the language setting.

Player names, scores and who won are never sent.

Google also records standard technical information such as an app-specific identifier, device model, operating system and app version, language, when and how long the app is used, and an approximate location (country or city) derived from your IP address. The app does not collect your advertising identifier, and this data is not used for advertising.

Event-level analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (by default, 2 months).

Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

Each time the app starts, it tells Google Firebase Crashlytics, so that we can see how often the app runs without a crash. If the app crashes or runs into an error, such as failing to open or save a game, it sends a report. Both contain technical details such as your device model, operating system and app version, and an identifier for the installation. A report also contains details of the error and the screens you visited just before it. Neither contains player names or scores. They help us fix problems and keep the app working. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Your permission in Europe

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your permission before it sends usage analytics or crash reports, and sends neither unless you allow it. Your consent is then the legal basis. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Usage and crash reports, and the app works the same either way.

If you say no, or change your answer to no, the app stops sending them and deletes the identifier and events that Analytics keeps on your device, and any crash reports still waiting there. On Android, crash reports stop from the next time the app starts.

Whether to ask is decided on your device, from its region setting and time zone. Because every European time zone counts, the app can also ask elsewhere in Europe. If you already use the app in another place and then open it in Europe, that one app start may be recorded before the app asks. In other places usage analytics and crash reports are on without asking.

Advertising

The app shows no ads. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Your choices

  • Your games: remove a game in History, or delete every saved game for good with Settings → Clear All History. Uninstalling the app also deletes them.
  • Usage and crash reports: where the app asks for permission, change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy → Usage and crash reports.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to object to its use. Because the app has no accounts, we generally cannot link analytics or crash data to you. Contact us at support@timsa7.com and we will help as far as we can. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. The services above are provided by Google, which processes the data on our behalf and under its own terms, also in other countries such as the United States. We share information with others only if the law requires it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. Questions: support@timsa7.com
