Super Trix Calculator – Support

Super Trix Calculator keeps score for Trix, the four-player card game, for individual players or two teams of partners. On your device it is called Trix Calculator.

Contact

For questions, problems or suggestions, email support@timsa7.com. Please include your device model and the app version, which is in Settings.

Common questions

How do I correct a score? Open the kingdom, tap the contract, change the numbers and save again. The standings update.

Why is a kingdom locked? Kingdoms are played in order. Finish all five contracts of the earlier kingdom first.

Why can't I save a contract? The numbers have to add up: 13 tricks, 13 diamonds, 4 queens, one player for the King of Hearts and a place for everyone in Trix. The screen shows what is missing.

Who are partners? Partners sit opposite each other: players 1 and 3 against players 2 and 4.

What is Complex Scoring? It puts Collection, Diamonds, King of Hearts and Queens on one screen. Trix is still entered on its own.

How do I get back to a game? Choose Continue Game on the first screen, or open the game in History.

Are my games sent anywhere? No. Player names, scores and saved games stay on your device. See the privacy policy.

Is there an Arabic version? Not yet.
